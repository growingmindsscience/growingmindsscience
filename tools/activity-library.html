  • Every activity names the developmental mechanism it feeds, not just "fun sensory play".

These activities are educational suggestions, not therapy or medical advice. You know your child; skip anything that doesn't fit their abilities today, and stay close for anything involving water, climbing, or food. Ages are guides, not rules.

How these are built

Each activity is written fresh for this library and passes an automated check before it can ship: required safety notes, a materials screen against choking and strangulation hazards for the age range, and one non-negotiable rule: the intention line must name a real developmental mechanism from a controlled vocabulary, so "boosts brain development" hand-waving can't get through. The library favors ordinary moments over projects; the research consistently finds the developmental value lives in the interaction, not the materials.
