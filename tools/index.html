Interactive tools

Hands-on tools to use with your child or on your own. Each one gives you something specific back.

Growing Minds AI

Any age · 5 free questions a day

Calm answers drawn from a curated research library. Included with the toddler class, with a fair-use limit of 50 questions a day.

Number Path

Ages 2 to 5 · 10 minutes

A ten-minute counting check-in at home that shows where your child is on the counting ladder, then matches games to it. The check-in is free.

Milestone tracker

Birth to 36 months

What’s typical at each age across six domains, drawn from ZERO TO THREE, Pathways.org, NAEYC and the Child Development Institute. Saved in your browser, printable for checkups.

Activity Library

Birth to 3 · Household materials

Activities with the “why” attached, ways to make each easier or harder, and a daily Today’s 3.

Reading Prompt Cards

18 months to 5 · Any book

Turn any children’s book into a back-and-forth with dialogic reading, an evidence-backed method. Printable as a deck.

Communication Snapshot

6 to 36 months · 4 minutes

A few careful questions about words, gestures and understanding, and printable notes if a pediatrician conversation is worth having.

Before your child’s checkup

Birth to 5 · 5 minutes

Write down what you’ve noticed and what you want to ask, then print one page for the visit. Never a score.
