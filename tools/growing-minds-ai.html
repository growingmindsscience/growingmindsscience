Why this is different

Not a generic parenting chatbot.

Growing Minds AI is being designed around evidence-based developmental science, SRCD-informed literature, and your Growing Minds Science course material. The goal is to give families better explanations, not more pressure or one-size-fits-all scripts.

Planned knowledge base

  • Growing Minds Science lessons and parent tools
  • Developmental psychology concepts in plain language
  • Research-backed early childhood guidance
  • Clear limits around medical, diagnostic, and crisis advice

Product principle

Helpful, bounded, and honest.

  • Answers should explain development, not diagnose a child.
  • Activities should be realistic for ordinary family life.
  • Uncertainty should be named clearly, especially when professional support may be needed.
