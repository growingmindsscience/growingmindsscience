Milestone Navigator

Worried about something specific: talking, walking, hearing, play? Pick the worry, answer a few plain questions, and get a clear, research-grounded next step, whether that's genuine reassurance or exactly what to say when you call.

Honest about its job

What this is, and isn't

  • It turns a vague worry into specific observations, and observations into a next step.
  • Reassurance here is real: it means nothing you reported crosses a published line.
  • It is not a screening or diagnostic instrument, and it never labels your child.
  • Everything stays on this device. Your answers are never sent to us.

This tool offers educational information, not medical advice, diagnosis, or screening. It cannot see your child; it can only organize what you report. Decisions about your child's health belong with you and your child's clinician. If your child is in immediate danger or distress, call your local emergency number.

How this works

Every path through this tool is checked against a set of published red lines, like not walking by 18 months (AAP motor-delay guidance) or caregiver concern about hearing at any age (Joint Committee on Infant Hearing). An automated check runs on every update to this site and enumerates every possible path: if any answer pattern that crosses a research line would land on a softer result than the research supports, the update fails and cannot ship. The tool can be more careful than the literature; it is never allowed to be less.

Loss of skills a child once had is treated as the most important observation at any age, in every category. And hearing is deliberately different from everything else here: your concern alone is enough for a hearing test, with no watch-and-wait step in between.
