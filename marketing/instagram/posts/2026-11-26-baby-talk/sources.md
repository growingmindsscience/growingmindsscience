# Sources

Verified for the 2026-08-19 carousel (built and verified, never posted); rebuilt here with the same claims.

- ManyBabies Consortium. Quantifying sources of variability in infancy research using the infant-directed-speech preference. *Advances in Methods and Practices in Psychological Science*. 2020;3(1):24-52. 67 labs, 2,329 infants, 3 to 15 months; d = 0.35; moderated by age, accent match and method.
- Zettersten M, et al. Evaluating the reliability of the infant-directed speech preference. *Open Mind*. 2024;8. Test-retest r ≈ 0.09.
- Schreiner MS, et al. Limited evidence of test-retest reliability in infant-directed speech preference, and a corrected meta-analysis. *Developmental Science*. 2024. The corrected and expanded meta-analysis also lands near d = 0.35.
- Soderstrom M, et al. Testing the relationship between preferences for infant-directed speech and vocabulary development. *Journal of Child Language*. 2024. No link to vocabulary at 18 or 24 months.
