# Sources

The correction is in the certified corpus (`self-regulation-marshmallow`, `knowledge/curated.mjs`), on the rejected-topics list in `marketing/carousels/daily-log.md` (as a claim never to run uncorrected), and was verified for LinkedIn draft 09 (2026-08-07).

- Mischel W, Shoda Y, Rodriguez ML. Delay of gratification in children. *Science*. 1989;244(4907):933-938. Original paradigm and the small Stanford Bing Nursery School follow-ups.
- Watts TW, Duncan GJ, Quan H. Revisiting the marshmallow test: a conceptual replication investigating links between early delay of gratification and later outcomes. *Psychological Science*. 2018;29(7):1159-1177. n = 918; the association with age-15 achievement was substantially smaller and largely attenuated by controls for family background and early cognitive ability.
- Mischel W, Ebbesen EB, Zeiss AR. Cognitive and attentional mechanisms in delay of gratification. *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology*. 1972;21(2):204-218. Strategy instructions (distraction, reframing) lengthened waiting substantially.
