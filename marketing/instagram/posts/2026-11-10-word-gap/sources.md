# Sources

The correction is already graded on the site (`claims/talking-to-baby.html`) and was verified for LinkedIn draft 03 (2026-07-24); the same sources are used here.

- Hart B, Risley TR. *Meaningful Differences in the Everyday Experience of Young American Children*. Brookes, 1995. 42 families, one hour of recording per month, extrapolated.
- Sperry DE, Sperry LL, Miller PJ. Reexamining the verbal environments of children from different socioeconomic backgrounds. *Child Development*. 2019;90(4):1303-1318. Whole-day recordings including all speakers; did not find the gap.
- Dailey S, Bergelson E. Language input to infants of different socioeconomic statuses: a quantitative meta-analysis. *Developmental Science*. 2022;25(3):e13192. A smaller difference that depends on what is counted.
- Romeo RR et al. Beyond the 30-million-word gap. *Psychological Science*. 2018;29(5):700-710. Romeo RR et al. *Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience*. 2021 (randomized trial).
