# Sources

- CDC. *Learn the Signs. Act Early.* Developmental milestone checklists, revised 2022. https://www.cdc.gov/ncbddd/actearly/ (corpus entry `milestones-are-ranges`).
- Zubler JM, Wiggins LD, Macias MM, et al. Evidence-informed milestones for developmental surveillance tools. *Pediatrics*. 2022;149(3):e2021052138. The 2022 revision lists milestones that 75% or more of children reach by the stated age; the 18-month language item is "tries to say three or more words besides mama or dada".
- Corpus entry `language-development` (`knowledge/curated.mjs`): first words often around the first birthday, combining words often in the second year, wide normal variation; interaction quality over exposure.
- Corpus entry `milestones-are-ranges`: the signs that merit a conversation (loss of skills, no response to name, very limited eye contact or social engagement, a consistent lag across several areas) and the early-intervention rationale.
