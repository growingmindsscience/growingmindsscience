# Sources

Verified from the full primary-source PDF for the 2026-08-28 carousel (built and verified, never posted); rebuilt here with the same claims.

- Lyons DE, Young AG, Keil FC. The hidden structure of overimitation. *PNAS*. 2007;104(50):19751-19756. 63 children aged 3 to 5; trained and praised for spotting unnecessary actions; odds ratio 147.0 for the puzzle box's irrelevant mechanism versus baseline; explicit warnings did not reduce copying; copying vanished when the box halves were physically separated.
- Hoehl S, Keupp S, Schleihauf H, McGuigan N, Buttelmann D, Whiten A. "Over-imitation": a review and appraisal of a decade of research. *Developmental Review*. 2019;51:90-108.
- Stengelin R, Hepach R, Haun DBM. Cross-cultural variation in how much, but not whether, children overimitate. *Journal of Experimental Child Psychology*. 2020;193:104796. doi:10.1016/j.jecp.2019.104796.
