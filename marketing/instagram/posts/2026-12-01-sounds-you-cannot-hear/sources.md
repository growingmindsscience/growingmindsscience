# Sources

Approved by Matthew on 2026-07-16 for the 2026-07-17 carousel (never posted); rebuilt here with the same claims.

- Werker JF, Tees RC. Cross-language speech perception: evidence for perceptual reorganization during the first year of life. *Infant Behavior and Development*. 1984;7(1):49-63. Thompson (Nlaka'pamux) velar/uvular contrast; 8 of 10 at 6 to 8 months, 1 of 10 at 10 to 12 months, 3 of 10 English-speaking adults; Hindi- and Thompson-learning infants retained their native contrasts at 11 to 12 months.
- Kuhl PK, Tsao F-M, Liu H-M. Foreign-language experience in infancy: effects of short-term exposure and social interaction on phonetic learning. *PNAS*. 2003;100(15):9096-9101. Twelve sessions with live Mandarin speakers preserved discrimination; audio-only and video-only exposure did not.
