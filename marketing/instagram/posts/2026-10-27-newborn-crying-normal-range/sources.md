- Wolke D, Bilgin A, Samara M (2017). Systematic review and meta-analysis: fussing and crying durations and prevalence of colic in infants. Journal of Pediatrics. DOI 10.1016/j.jpeds.2017.02.020 (28 diary studies, 8,690 infants).
- Vermillet et al. (2022). Child Development. DOI 10.1111/cdev.13760 (57 studies, 17 countries). Supports the same card (f3), including the 'no single sharp peak' line.
- Cabana et al. (2020), a small recorder study (N = 20) that measured far less crying than parent diaries; cited from the confidence note on card f3, no DOI given.
- St James-Roberts I, Conroy S, Wilsher K (1998). Links between maternal care and persistent infant crying in the early months. Child: Care, Health and Development 24(5):353-376. DOI 10.1046/j.1365-2214.2002.00089.x (the 2002 in the DOI is the date it was posted online).
- St James-Roberts I, Peachey E (2011). Archives of Disease in Childhood. DOI 10.1136/adc.2010.200204.
- Rothbart MK, Sheese BE, Rueda MR, Posner MI (2011). Developing mechanisms of self-regulation in early life. Emotion Review. DOI 10.1177/1754073910387943.
- Wesarg-Menzel et al. (2023). Developmental Review. DOI 10.1016/j.dr.2023.101090 (meta-review of 136 reviews and meta-analyses). Supports card f8.
- Growing Minds class, Birth to 12 Months, Lesson 1.4 (Matthew's teaching script): practical suggestions and the 'call your pediatrician' signs, which the lesson labels standard pediatric guidance, not a research finding.
