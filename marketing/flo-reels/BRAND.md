# How the Reels apply the brand guide

Source: From Little Onward Brand Identity and Experience Guidelines, version 2.1 (October 2026). Where this note and the guide differ, the guide wins.

## Type
- Newsreader for headlines and the hook (500 for the hero line, 400 for beat headlines, italic for a pull quote or the trust line). Inter for captions, labels and small metadata.
- Fonts come from the site's own files in `assets/fonts/` (both SIL OFL). Lora and Instrument Sans were withdrawn in version 2.1 and are not used.

## Color (`--flo-*` tokens)
| Token | Hex | Use in a Reel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--flo-ink` | #193C39 | Dark grounds, text on paper |
| `--flo-paper` | #F7F4EC | Light grounds |
| `--flo-white` | #FFFDF8 | Text on ink, cards |
| `--flo-sage` | #E0EBE5 | Explanatory panels |
| `--flo-verdigris` | #245F58 | One action or highlight per frame |
| `--flo-ochre` | #D2A453 | Small discovery accents only (a dot, a rule, a numeral). Never small white text on it |
| `--flo-clay` | #C46C4D | Annotation marks and small eyebrows only, never body text |
| `--flo-research` | #426B91 | Source lines |
| `--flo-leaf` | #8DAE9A | The mark only |

Proportions as guidance: about 70% paper and white, 20% ink and verdigris, 10% clay, ochre and blue.

## Frames
- 1080 x 1920. Text stays between y 250 and y 1520 (out of the top 250 px and bottom 400 px).
- One idea per frame, at most 8 words on screen. Generous whitespace, quiet rules, small figure-style metadata ("FIG. 02 / 6 TO 12 MONTHS").
- Motion is calm and purposeful: short fades, small slides, a slow zoom. Nothing moves only to look dynamic.
- The mark is `assets/img/brand/mark.svg` (on paper) or `mark-dark.svg` (on ink), with clear space of at least the leaf's width. No other sprouts, trees, brains, footprints, growth charts or puzzle pieces.
- Illustration explains a mechanism (an annotated scene, a simple process diagram). No identifiable children, no stock smiling families, no copyrighted characters.

## Words
- From Little Onward in title case in copy; "a From Little Onward class". One identity line per surface at most.
- Trust line: "Clear about what's known. Honest about what's not." Say "linked to" unless the study shows cause.
- No em dashes anywhere.
