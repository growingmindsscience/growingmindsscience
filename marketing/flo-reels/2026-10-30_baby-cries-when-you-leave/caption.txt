If your baby melts down when you step out of the room, you're not doing anything wrong. Separation anxiety is a common, expected part of the first year and often into toddlerhood.

It tends to show up as babies learn that people keep existing when they can't see them, and as they figure out who they count on most.

What helps: keep goodbyes short and warm, use the same phrase each time ("Bye bye, I'll be right back"), and always come back when you said you would. Games like peekaboo let them practice the leaving and the return.
