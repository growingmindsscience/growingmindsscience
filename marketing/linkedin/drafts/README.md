plus Kuhl perceptual-narrowing pair. Those are the exact topics of the 2026-07-16 and 2026-07-17

1. `daily-log.md` maintains a "topics already used, do not repeat" ledger. It is the canonical

   month open with two repeats, which reads as a thin content pipeline rather than a deep one.

   is a waste of them. They earn a fresh slot in a later month where they can lead.

schedule whenever you want them, with dates removed. Recommend September or later, and log them in
`daily-log.md` when they run.

**Correction made to post 13.** The earlier draft said the ManyBabies4 replication ran "with 1,018
infants," taken from `daily-log.md`. The published record is more specific: 1,018 infants tested
across 37 labs on five continents, 567 meeting inclusion criteria for the primary analysis. The
post now carries both figures and the actual result (50.89% helper choice in the social condition,
not different from chance; 55.85% in the nonsocial condition, also not different from chance, nor
from the social condition). The headline still says 1,018, which is the number tested and is
accurate.

**Correction made to post 11.** The earlier draft claimed children with "warmer, more
autonomy-supportive" parents showed more committed compliance. The Kochanska line of work measures
relationship warmth and mutual responsiveness, not autonomy support specifically. The post now
states what was measured and explicitly flags the autonomy reading as an extension rather than a
finding.

**Nothing was invented to fill a gap.** Where a detail could not be confirmed from a primary or
publisher source it was cut rather than estimated. Specifically: the Wood, Bruner & Ross sample
size is not stated in post 04, because the figure appears only in secondary summaries and the
paper itself was not retrievable (publisher 403, and the open PDF mirror was corrupt). The post
describes the ages and the task, which are consistently reported, and does not give an n.

Hart & Risley (1995) is cited by year only, as a book rather than an article, which is correct.
