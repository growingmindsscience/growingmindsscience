# 14 · What is free on the site, and why

- **Suggested post date:** Wednesday, 2026-08-19

- **Related site pages:** /tools, /articles, /tools/growing-minds-ai

Occasionally someone asks what the catch is on the free material at Growing Minds Science. There is not one, but there is a reason, and it is worth stating plainly.

Most of what a parent needs at 11pm is not a course. It is one accurate answer to one specific question, right now, without a signup wall or a countdown timer or a chapter of preamble.

So the following are free and stay free:

**Growing Minds AI.** A chat assistant grounded in a curated research corpus rather than the open internet. Ask it the actual question you have. It will tell you when something is contested rather than picking a side to sound confident.

**The Communication Snapshot.** A few minutes, no account, a plain-language read on whether a child's communication is tracking in the typical range or is worth raising with a pediatrician.

**The article library.** Longer explainers on serve and return, the toddler "no", self-regulation, screen time, and the rest. Each one includes where the research is solid and where it has been over-applied, because leaving that part out is how good findings turn into bad advice.

**The milestone tracker.** Simple, and yours.

The paid classes exist and I would be glad if they are useful to you. But they are for people who want the long version, not a toll gate on the short one.

Everything above is at growingmindsscience.com.

*Educational content, not medical advice. Not a diagnostic instrument, and not a substitute for your pediatrician.*
