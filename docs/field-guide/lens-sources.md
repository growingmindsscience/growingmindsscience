## Could not verify

- **The 87%, 91% and 59% tantrum rates by age:** these were read in Hoyniak 2023, which attributes them to Potegal & Davidson 2003. They are not in the Potegal abstract. The proposed L1 wording does not print them.
- **Abstracts not available:** Frye et al. 1995, Zelazo et al. 1996, Kuczynski & Kochanska 1990 (read via a university repository copy) and Kochanska & Aksan 1995 (read via a repository summary). Their citations are confirmed.
- **No direct evidence was found for:**
  - narrating the next step improving transitions (L15)
  - "fewer words, a lower voice" shortening tantrums (L16)
  - advance notice helping typical toddlers in everyday settings (L13). The only studies are small single-case designs, mostly negative.
