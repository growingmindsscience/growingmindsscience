# Brand guidelines, as they apply to the build

Distilled from `Growing_Minds_Science_Brand_Guidelines.pdf` (ten pages, "Field Notes & Discovery", supplied 2026-10-14). The PDF is the authority; this file records what the build has to honour and where it differs from what shipped in Phases 1 and 2.

## The one filter

Every substantial design decision answers: does this help someone observe more carefully, understand more accurately, respond more constructively, or reflect more usefully?

Proportions: 70% paper and white, 20% forest and verdigris, 10% clay, ochre and blue. Illustrations may use the accents selectively; deep greens are for authority and structure.

Contrast the guidelines state: ink on paper about 10.9:1; white on verdigris about 7.4:1; research blue on paper about 5.1:1; clay on paper about 3.4:1 and white on clay about 3.8:1, so clay never carries body text; ochre never carries small white text.

What shipped in Phases 1 and 2 used the brief's earlier proposals (`#20322F`, `#F7F5EF`, `#315D52`, `#A9C3B2`). The values are close; the plan's first step reconciles them. Sage mist is far lighter than the shipped sage field, so the field sections need a decision (see plan, checkpoint C0).

## Type

Preferred: Newsreader for display and Inter for interface and reading. Named second direction: Instrument Serif with DM Sans. The site ships Newsreader with DM Sans. Either pairing is inside the guidelines; checkpoint C0 settles it.

Wordmark direction: lowercase "growing minds" with a widely tracked "SCIENCE" descriptor. Type before symbol. No tree, sprout, brain or puzzle-piece marks; if a motif is wanted, an observation motif (overlapping fields of view, a calibrated lens, an annotation mark).

Layout: generous whitespace, quiet rules, strong editorial grids, small figure numbering, calm rounded corners, occasional edge annotations. No stacked heavy coloured cards, no ornamental icons filling space.

## Messaging architecture

Four lines, one job each. Never all four on one screen; one lead message and one supporting thought per surface.

| Line | Job | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| See what's growing. | Primary tagline | Logo lockups, homepage hero, campaigns |
| The first five years, understood. | Educational promise | Course pages, editorial intros, platform descriptions |
| Mental models, not scripts. | Teaching philosophy | About page, pedagogy, product reviews |
| Less pressure. Better explanations. | Parent-centred promise | Reassurance, benefit statements, onboarding |

Supporting language, never a primary line: "There's more to every moment." (editorial campaign), "Understand first. Respond better." (practical learning), "Clear about what's known. Honest about what's not." (sources and evidence features).

## Art direction

Photography is observational: real families, ordinary rooms, exploratory gestures, concentration, natural light. No generic smiling stock, no immaculate homes, no exaggerated distress, no brain imagery.

Illustration reveals mechanisms: annotated scenes, relationship maps, developmental timelines, process diagrams, small multiples. One reusable grammar: precise labels, restrained arrows, consistent stroke weights, meaningful accents, figure captions. Conceptual explanations are labelled conceptual.

Three signature moves: annotated observation (a naturalistic moment, labels on request), the explanatory reveal (a simple model introduced progressively), field-guide notation (ages, topics, figure IDs, sources as quiet metadata).

Motion discloses relationships, differences, sequences or a new level of explanation. Quick, low amplitude, reduced-motion always supported. Never movement for its own sake.

The visual test: would it still read as Growing Minds Science with the logo removed?

## Verbal identity

Reveal, don't prescribe. Illuminate, don't oversimplify. Empower, don't pressure. Intelligent not academic; warm not sentimental; useful not rigid; confident about evidence without manufacturing certainty. Differentiate observation, evidence-supported explanation, practical options and individual professional advice. Never invent research, citations, effects or consensus labels.

## The Developmental Lens

Observe, Understand, Respond, Reflect, as built. Component requirements the guidelines add to the brief: age and topic metadata, evidence references, contextual links to classes and tools, an accessible text alternative to the visual, and never forcing the sequence on a parent who needs help now.

## Website expression

Navigation: Explore, Classes, Tools, Articles, About, with Growing Minds AI and My classes as utility actions and a recognisable shortcut to hard-moment support.

Three entry points: by age, by moment, by topic. These are three doors into shared content, not three collections.

Homepage order (as built): hero, Lens preview, age exploration, three dimensions, hard moments, classes, tools and evidence, trust and invitation.

Preservation: every existing course, access and payment flow, Growing Minds AI, Number Path, Milestone Tracker, Communication Snapshot, activities, reading prompts, hard-moment support, arcade, articles, guides and the evidence-graded claims stay.

## Approval checklist (the guidelines' own)

- Brand recognisable without a logo; no generic parenting visuals.
- One lead message per surface; the four lines used in their roles.
- The interface creates understanding, not only visual novelty.
- Research uncertainty stays honest; no unsupported clinical claims.
- A mobile parent in a hurry finds immediate support quickly.
- Purchased classes and tools remain fully accessible.
