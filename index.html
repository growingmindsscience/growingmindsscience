My toddler says "no" to everything. Is something wrong?
Not at all — this is actually a healthy sign. Between 18 months and 3 years, toddlers are actively building autonomy. "No" is their way of practicing self-determination while their prefrontal cortex is still very immature. The key is to offer real choices where you can, and hold the line calmly where you need to.
So I shouldn't try to stop it?
The goal isn't to stop it — it's to channel it. When you give choices ("Do you want the red cup or the blue cup?"), the toddler gets to say yes to something, which satisfies the autonomy drive without a battle.
