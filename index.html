A developmental-science education for parents

Understand your child.Understand yourself.Understand your influence.

Self-paced classes in child development and developmental psychology for parents of children 0 to 5, built from peer-reviewed research. The understanding that used to require a graduate program, finished in evenings instead of semesters.

Browse the classes How it’s taught ↓

Taught by Matthew McArthur, Child Development Specialist. M.A. in Developmental Psychology (SDSU), B.A. in Psychology (UCLA), and 100+ families coached in Los Angeles.

The parents who do best are, almost without exception, the ones who understand the science.

Matthew McArthur, founder

Six years studying developmental psychology and two years coaching families in Los Angeles taught me one consistent thing. Parents who know how development works stay calmer, read their children more accurately, and change the things that actually matter. Not because they memorized scripts, but because they can see what is happening in front of them.

That knowledge has lived inside graduate programs and academic journals. It should not require either. Growing Minds Science is the course in child development every parent should have been offered, taught for people with a job, a household, and about forty minutes after bedtime.

Every class teaches you to see three things.

Your child
What is being built at this age, what is typical and what is not, and why the hardest behaviors are so often the development itself, showing up on schedule.
Yourself
How your own nervous system, history, and stress enter the room, and what regulation actually looks like in an adult, because a child’s calm is borrowed before it is owned.
Your influence
Which parts of the environment shape development (conversation, routines, serve and return) and which worries the research lets you set down for good.

Taught like a seminar. Built for a Tuesday night.

Short video lessons with full transcripts, watched in any order at any hour. The research is shown rather than hidden, and the goal is a mental model you can carry, not a script to recite.

  • Self-paced, lifetime access
  • Full transcripts
  • Sources cited
  • Growing Minds AI included

  • Translate the research, don’t replace it

    Plain language, with honesty about what the evidence is and is not. No invented certainty, no oversimplified hooks.

  • Mental models over scripts

    A clear way to understand what is happening travels to the situations no script anticipated. Scripts don’t.

  • Respect the parent

    You are an adult who is already trying hard and already paying attention. The work assumes nuance, not compliance.

  • Calm by default

    No urgency manufactured for engagement, no shame. Educational content, and never a substitute for medical advice.

  • Built for ordinary days

    Every recommendation has to survive a normal weekday, between transitions, meals, work, and the real capacity of tired adults.

Before you enroll

Can I try the approach before I pay?

Yes. The articles, the free tools, and Growing Minds AI are written and built the same way the classes are taught, with no account needed. If they fit your family, the classes will.

Do I need any background in psychology?

No. The classes assume you are intelligent and busy, not that you have read the literature. Every term is explained the first time it appears.

How long do I have access?

For life. One payment, no subscription, no deadlines. Watch on your phone, tablet, or laptop, in any order.

Can I get a refund?

All sales are final, which is why everything free on this site is written in the same voice as the classes: use it first. If something goes wrong with your access, get in touch and it goes directly to Matthew.

Is this medical or psychological advice?

No. The classes are educational. For concerns about your own child, your pediatrician and your child’s clinicians are the right people to ask, and the classes will help you ask better questions.
