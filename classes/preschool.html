By the end of the class

You’ll understand what your preschooler is actually building. Clear about what’s known. Honest about what’s not.

  • What changes in the preschool brain between ages 3 and 5, and why your child can still fall apart over small things.
  • What self-regulation actually is, how it develops, and how adults support it without doing it for the child.
  • Why child-directed play is doing more developmental work than most structured activities.
  • How preschool social skills develop, and what to do (and not do) when children fight or exclude.
  • What kindergarten readiness research actually measures, and why executive function matters far more than academic skills.

Who teaches it.

Created and taught by Matthew McArthur, who holds an M.A. in Developmental Psychology from San Diego State University, where he studied how parent stress, changes at home, screen time, and back-and-forth interaction shape language and thinking from 6 months to 5 years, after working with developmental researchers at UCLA, and he wrote “Why Early Chatter Matters” for Frontiers for Young Minds.

He then spent two years in state-funded early intervention, coaching families with children under 3 who were experiencing delays.

Matthew writes and reviews every lesson. The narration is an AI model of his own voice, reading his script word for word.

A few things parents usually ask first.

How do I start, and how do I get back in?

When you enroll, you create a free account, pay securely with Stripe, and the class opens right away. After that it lives in My classes, which remembers where you left off in every lesson.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Within 14 days of buying, and as long as you’ve watched 4 lessons or fewer, you get a full refund: email Matthew with the address you bought with. A refund ends your access to the class.

Is this medical or psychological advice?

No. The class is educational. For concerns about your own child, your pediatrician and your child’s clinicians are the right people to ask, and the class will help you ask better questions.
