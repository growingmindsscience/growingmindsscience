Questions parents ask

Which class includes the AI tool?

The toddler class, and all three classes when you enroll in them together. Ask the research, the AI tool, is included with either, with a fair-use limit of 50 questions a day. Without a class, Ask the research answers five free questions a day, with no account and no card.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Email Matthew with the address you bought with.

  • Classes: a full refund within 14 days of buying, as long as you have watched 4 lessons or fewer. The free preview lesson does not count toward the four. A refund ends your access to that class.
  • Tallykin: a full refund within 14 days of buying it.
  • Gift codes: a refund within 14 days of buying, if the code has not been redeemed.

The full wording is in the terms of sale.

Is any of this medical advice?

No. Everything here explains developmental science in plain language. It does not diagnose, label, or replace your pediatrician, therapist, or other specialists. More about the approach.
