Questions parents ask

What's the difference between the toddler class and AI Pro?

The toddler class is a one-time $49 payment that includes the full class and unlimited Growing Minds AI for life. AI Pro is just the unlimited AI, billed monthly. If you have a toddler, the class is the better deal; if you only want the AI, AI Pro is simpler.

Can I get a refund?

Not for a class: all class sales are final, which is why the free tools and articles are written the way the classes are taught. Use them first. AI Pro is month to month, so you can cancel any time and keep access through the period you've paid for. If something goes wrong with your access, get in touch.

What is Number Path?

A parent-run counting check-in for ages 2–5, plus weekly games and daily number talk matched to where your child is on the counting ladder. The check-in itself is free; the full version is one payment of $34. It's an enrichment tool, not a medical or developmental screening.

Do I need an account to try the AI?

No. The free tier gives you five questions every day with no sign-up and no card. It resets each midnight.

Can I cancel the monthly plan anytime?

Yes. AI Pro is month to month and you can cancel in one click. You keep access through the end of the period you've paid for.

I subscribed. How do I unlock unlimited access?

Right after checkout you're sent back to Growing Minds AI and access unlocks automatically. On a new device, open the unlock panel and confirm the email you subscribed with.

Is any of this medical advice?

No. Everything here explains developmental science in plain language. It does not diagnose, label, or replace your pediatrician, therapist, or other specialists. More about the approach.
