Questions parents ask

What is Number Path?

A parent-run counting check-in for ages 2–5, plus weekly games and daily number talk matched to where your child is on the counting ladder. One payment of $34. It's an enrichment tool, not a medical or developmental screening.

Do I need an account to try it?

No. The free tier gives you five questions every day with no sign-up and no card. It resets each midnight.

Can I cancel the monthly plan anytime?

Yes. AI Pro is month-to-month and you can cancel in one click. You keep access through the end of the period you've paid for.

What's the difference between the class bundle and AI Pro?

The class bundle is a one-time $49 payment that includes the full toddler class and unlimited Growing Minds AI for life. AI Pro is just the unlimited AI, billed monthly — best if you don't need the class.

Is this medical advice?

No. Growing Minds AI explains developmental science in plain language. It does not diagnose, label, or replace your pediatrician, therapist, or other specialists. More about the tool.

Is my conversation private?

Your conversation stays in your browser. We don't ask for personal details to use the free tier.
