01

Colours

A cool survey palette: one sheet, one ink, two drawn fields, one route. Teal is the only chromatic colour at text or button size. Values are read live from tokens-terrain.css, so the hex under each swatch is what the page is actually painting in this theme.

02

Typography

Geologica, a wide geometric sans, is the legend hand: anything that labels, counts or acts. Literata, an optical-size serif, is the reading hand: anything that explains. A number in Literata or a paragraph in Geologica is a mistake.

03

Rules, bars and frames

Depth is tonal and typographic. A panel announces itself with a 3px ink rule across the top. A class row or legend cell carries a 6px route segment: slate at rest, teal when active, dashed slate while in development. Photographs sit in a 2px ink frame like map insets. Nothing casts a shadow at rest.

Legend bar3px solid ink · panels, lists, figures, hr
Route bar, active6px solid teal · active cell or row
Route bar, at rest6px solid contour
Route bar, in development6px dashed contour
Inset frame2px solid ink · photographs
Control stroke1.5px hairline · inputs, tables, tags

06

Fields and the scale

Square 48px fields with a 1.5px hairline and a Geologica label above. Focus turns the border teal under a 2.5px outline. Errors are the one warm hue on the site. The age scale is a real range input: a 6px ink track and a 22px square teal thumb in a 2px ink border.

07

Navy fields

Two inverted navy fields close each page the way the dark field closes a sheet: the office-hours band on Navy Field, the enrol band and footer on Navy Floor. Headings on navy are pure white; the phrase mark becomes Navy Raised with white text; the chat demo is a surface card under a 3px teal rule.

Is it normal that my 2-year-old says no to everything?

Yes, and it is a good sign. Around two, children discover they are a separate person with a will of their own. Saying no is how they practise that.

08

The sheet, as an inset

Inner pages reuse the homepage's material as a terrain inset: the same contour, woodland, stream, route and waymark classes at inset weights. Photographs are square insets in a 2px ink frame; a captioned figure or an inset comes before a photograph when the section explains something.

09

Motion

Everything moves on one ease. Transforms, opacity and stroke-dashoffset only; never layout. Reveals are an enhancement, never a gate: content is visible by default and settles upward, so a slow paint or a screenshot never meets a blank hero.

House easecubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1), 240ms for colour and transform
PressButtons move down 1px on :active, 140ms. No hover lift.
Route draw-in1800ms stroke-dashoffset, once on load when html.anim is set.
Settle720ms translateY(14px) → 0, staggered 80ms; travel only, never a fade.
Menu200ms slide down 6px on cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1).
Reduced motionEvery animation and transition is removed; the route is drawn, the copy is in place.
