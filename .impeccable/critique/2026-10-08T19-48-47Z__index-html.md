## Anti-Patterns Verdict

LLM: homepage passes (ink hero with a cited Fig. 1, Besley over Atkinson, one amber highlighter, ruled rows; no absolute bans). Class pages are the most template-shaped surface: seven equal-size display H2s, two check-lists, no ink band. Copy tells: "X, not Y" / "rather than" ~8 times on the homepage, "scripts" as the foil 5 times, stacked two-beat aphorism headings. Biggest brand break: the /nsc free lesson and account step (Bricolage Grotesque, Source Serif 4, pill buttons, uppercase tracked eyebrow "FREE LESSON · MODULE 1", terracotta "CLASSES", cream poster).

Detector: CLI 9 findings, all single-font (false positive; regex stops at the first &; computed styles show Atkinson on 187 elements and Besley on 54). Browser overlay injected headless on 7 loads: dark-glow x4 and gpt-thin-border-wide-shadow on .chat and .nav__menu match DESIGN.md tokens; low-contrast on the nav (real 5.85:1) and the opt-in visual-contrast pass (real 5.17-14.8:1) are false positives; text-overflow on the rotated "Arousal" label is a false positive. Real (minor): nested-cards on the homepage phone menu points at a specificity leak: .site-header--ink .nav__menu (0,3,0) overrides the phone reset (0,2,0), leaving a drop shadow under the Free tools submenu in the drawer. Line length: no detector finding (its estimate is under its trigger), but measured characters per rendered line are 85-94 in class FAQ answers and 76-94 in the pricing FAQ: a 62ch cap is ~88 characters of Atkinson text.

## Priority Issues

1. [P1] The free lesson's destination undercuts the trust it exists to build. /nsc/classes/infant/preview switches brands (Bricolage, Source Serif, pills, uppercase eyebrow, terracotta, a cream poster whose play button covers the subtitle), and its "Read this lesson" is an unedited speech-to-text transcript: "Tronic/Tronix" for Tronick (6x), "parenties/Parentees" for parentese (10x), "Patricia Kool" for Kuhl (2x), "a coup" for coo (8x), a leading caption dash "- Welcome back." Fix: proofread the transcript and add headings and a sources list; restyle the public /nsc pages (preview, class-login, class-signup) with Seminar tokens. Command: /impeccable polish, then /impeccable clarify.
2. [P1] "My classes" is wrong for half the open catalogue. Signed-out visitors land on /nsc/class-login: "Your classes and lesson progress live in your Growing Minds Science account", no Thinkific mention, "Create an account". Fix: add "Took the toddler class? It's on Thinkific. Log in there" to the sign-in page; until the on-site move ships, make header "My classes" a two-row chooser. Command: /impeccable harden.
3. [P2] The free lesson sits below the fold where it matters. Hero actions are "Browse the classes" (duplicated by the header CTA) and "How it's taught"; on a 375x812 phone the free lesson first appears at 1,335px; on the infant page "Watch a lesson free" starts 9px below the fold because the four facts stack vertically; the strip's free row sits under the toddler row with no "infant" label. Fix: hero second action "Watch a lesson free · 14 min"; open-now strip before Fig. 1 on phones; relabel and reorder the strip row; 2x2 facts on phones. Command: /impeccable layout.
4. [P2] Flat heading scale; class pages lose the Seminar look. Homepage desktop H1 43.8px vs section H2s 49.6px; class pages H1 49.6px vs seven H2s at 46px (1.08x); "Who teaches it" over a two-line bio at display size; narrow sections jump the left edge 72px to 232px; no ink band on class pages. Fix: supporting sections to the Title step; H1 >= 1.25x H2; class enroll band on ink-900 with an amber CTA and the highlighter on the price. Command: /impeccable typeset, then /impeccable bolder.
5. [P2] The homepage is long and repeats itself before the price. Phone 14,512px, enrol band at 11,845px (14.6 screens); credentials 3x before the curriculum; the approach argued 3x; a 12-link shelf right before the price. Fix: instructor block to portrait + one line + link; merge the principles into the thesis; shelves to 3 links or after the enrol band; target <= 9,000px on phone. Command: /impeccable distill.

## Questions to Consider

- If "Serve and Return" is your best salesperson, why is the hero's second action "How it's taught"?
- Which brand is the product: Seminar or the /nsc app? A parent meets both in one session, and the app is what they see at the moment of payment.
- What would a 6-screen phone homepage keep? Does the approach need arguing three times before the price?
