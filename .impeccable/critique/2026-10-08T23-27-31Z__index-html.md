## Anti-Patterns Verdict

LLM: not slop. Besley over Atkinson confirmed loaded everywhere, ink bands, captioned figures with named sources, ruled rows, no icon-card grids, no eyebrows, no side stripes, no gradient text, no phone overflow on five pages. The live /nsc pages now match: Atkinson 17px body, Besley 500 H1, 48px buttons at 10px radius, sentence-case labels, ink poster with a real Play button; the transcript reads Tronick, parentese, Kuhl and coo correctly. Residue is in rhythm: 14 "X, not Y" constructions in 1,469 homepage words; the your child / yourself / your influence triad three times; "After this class you can explain" verbatim on all four units; seven homepage H2s at one size, four with the highlighter; amber radial washes at home.css 379, 739, 976; the scripted chat with a typing indicator is the stock AI-product trope, though labelled "Example conversation".

Detector (CLI): 7 findings, all single-font, all false positives (the regex stops at the first & in the Google Fonts link; both families are defined and used). Browser overlay on 4 local pages plus a phone pass: dark-glow x4 on the amber CTAs and gpt-thin-border-wide-shadow on .chat are real as reported but match DESIGN.md tokens (tight under-glow, negative spread); low-contrast on the ink header nav is a false positive (real 5.86:1, the detector used the body colour as background); nav__menu shadow hits are on closed details elements; the infant Enroll "hairline border" is the same colour as the fill. Real: line-length on .sources__lede (92 cpl) and borderline on .unit__fine (80 to 82 cpl). The live /nsc page refuses the overlay script by Content Security Policy (script-src 'self'), so no overlay there. The overlay is live in the [Human] tab on the homepage.

## Priority Issues

1. [P1] The Thinkific split still lands at the moment of paying. "Thinkific" appears 4 times on the homepage and 3 on /classes/; the enrol band does three jobs (buy class 1 on-site, buy class 2 off-site, join two waitlists) with six actions; "My classes" signed out shows a third-party sign-in with an arrow. Why: the one place a skeptical parent decides whether to trust you with a card is where the system visibly forks. Fix: collapse "My classes" to one link to /nsc/class-login (that page already explains the toddler case); on the toddler row say "Checkout opens in a new tab" and keep the platform name to fine print; move the two waitlists to one ruled row above the FAQ so the enrol rows are the last beat. Command: /impeccable harden, then /impeccable distill.
2. [P1] Measure overruns against the 70-character cap in the paragraphs a tired reader actually reads. .unit__promise 89 cpl (home and /classes/), .syllabus__body p 87, .sources-list__cite 102, .sources__lede 92, .unit__fine 123, .classes-list__note 101, pricing lede 100, pricing FAQ 83. Fix: max-width 50ch on each of those selectors; keep the grid, cap the text node. Command: /impeccable typeset.
3. [P2] Credential copy stacks degrees and never names the work. "M.A. in Developmental Psychology (SDSU), B.A. in Psychology (UCLA), and more than 100 families" runs in the hero, the thesis instructor block and the class page; PRODUCT.md forbids credential-stacking. The actual proof is absent everywhere: research at SDSU on how parent stress, home-environment change, screen time and reciprocal interaction shape language and cognition from 6 months to 5 years; work with UCLA developmental researchers; a published parent-facing article in Frontiers for Young Minds ("Why early chatter matters"); state-funded early-intervention coaching for families with under-3s experiencing delays. Fix: one sentence in the hero naming the research and the early-intervention work; degrees once, on About, with the article linked as a sample of the teaching. Command: /impeccable clarify.
4. [P2] Pricing page hierarchy is flat and inverted. FAQ H2 46px equals the H1 at 46.98px; group H2s (Free / Classes / Also available) 24.8px equal the price-name H3s; the $ amount at 34px outranks both; "Subscribe, $9 a month" shares the ghost style with "Meet Number Path", which is not a purchase. Fix: group and FAQ H2s at the title step (34px), price names 24.8px, amounts 28px; one button style for purchases. Command: /impeccable typeset, then /impeccable layout.
5. [P2] Phone pages are still long and end on the wrong beat. Homepage 9,971px ending on the waitlist form; class page 10,862px with the enrol band at 11 screens and the syllabus about 3,400px fully expanded; the open phone drawer is 513px tall so the hero's amber buttons render under the menu's amber Enroll; the fixed enroll bar (77px) has no dismiss.
