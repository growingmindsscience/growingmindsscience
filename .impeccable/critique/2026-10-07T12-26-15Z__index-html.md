## Anti-Patterns Verdict
LLM: mostly not AI-looking. Tells: identical rise-and-fade reveal on every section; the chat demo is the default AI-product mockup; six two-column sections in a row. Highlighter mark and figures read as a real system.
Detector CLI: 1 finding (single-font) = false positive (scanner does not resolve var(--font-body)). Browser: 8 findings; real = skipped heading (h1 -> h3), 64px shadow blur with 1px border on .chat and .signup__form, amber glow under 3 CTAs (intentional), tight leading on pull quote (display size, acceptable); low-contrast on "Free tools" = false positive (detector read through the translucent header to the body ground).

## Priority Issues
- [P0] Tablet curriculum rows (721-1040px): .unit__side 3-column collapse makes meta wrap one word per line and buttons overlap price/status.
- [P1] No purchase reassurance: no refund language, no preview, no "what happens after you pay". Repo policy: infant class says all sales final; toddler defers to Thinkific. Must be honest.
- [P1] Closing band splits intent: "Browse the classes" vs 3-field waitlist form plus email-cadence FAQs. Peak-end lands on products that don't exist.
- [P2] Instructor and proof are footnotes: 48px avatar + one sentence. No testimonials exist in repo; do not fabricate.
- [P2] Heading order: thesis section labelled by a <p>, outline skips H1 -> H3.
- [P3] Same reveal on every section.
