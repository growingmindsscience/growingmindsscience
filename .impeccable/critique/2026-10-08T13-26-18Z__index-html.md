## Anti-Patterns Verdict

LLM: mostly not slop. The captioned, sourced figures ("Narrower, still open", "Words land here", the crying curve), founder portrait, real-citation chat demo, ruled rows and price sheet carry a point of view. Tells at the edges: the three-part "Understand X." hero, a kicker above every hero, the same Besley-H2-plus-gray-lede section head ~10 times, arrow-ended rows, "After this class you can explain" bolded four times, the generic SaaS spot illustration on /tools/, and /about/ (stock mission-statement H1, inline styles, retired .eyebrow).

Detector: CLI 7 findings, all single-font (detector bug: reads only the first Google Fonts family). Browser overlay on 6 pages: dark-glow x4 on the amber buttons (documented button-amber token), gpt-thin-border-wide-shadow on .chat and the closed .nav__menu (documented lg and md tokens), low-contrast on "Free tools" in run 2 only (detector cannot parse oklab; real ratio ~8.4:1), clipped-overflow in run 1 only (visually hidden labels). Real: line-length on class pages (.check-list li 816px, .educator p 724px, FAQ answers 749px, all 17px, ~85-108ch) and pricing FAQ answers (114-128ch). Since the last run the detector no longer finds numbered markers, the 999px radius, oversized article H1s, or line-length on /classes/ rows. Also seen: styles.css --shadow-lg differs from DESIGN.md's lg token; .nav__menu adds an undocumented second shadow layer.

## Priority Issues

1. [P1] Split checkout is disclosed in prose, not in the controls, and the return path breaks. Homepage .enrol-item__cta shows two identical amber "Enroll" buttons; Class 2 opens matthew-s-site-de0b.thinkific.com in a new tab, with the cue only in sr-only text and a paragraph below. After purchase, "My classes" (/nsc/app/classes) has no Thinkific class. Fix: send homepage Class 2 to /classes/toddlerhood#enroll (or label "Enroll on Thinkific"), add a visible "Toddler class? Log in on Thinkific" link near My classes and on /nsc/app/classes until the migration ships. Command: /impeccable clarify.
2. [P1] Figure text is still illegible on phones outside Fig. 1. Homepage Fig. 2 (.fig--loop, 15 units on a 460 viewBox) renders ~10px at 375; article figures (720 viewBox, 13.5-16 units, Besley) render ~7-8px. Breaks DESIGN.md's 14px Figure Rule. Fix: apply the .fig--plate phone step-up to .fig--loop; redraw article figures on a 480 viewBox or move annotations into real text; labels in Atkinson. Command: /impeccable typeset.
3. [P1] No way to sample the teaching before a final-sale purchase. "Can I try the approach before I pay?" points to articles. Fix: publish one real lesson per open class (e.g. infant Module 1, "Serve and Return") as video + transcript; make "Watch a lesson free" the secondary class-page CTA. Command: /impeccable onboard.
4. [P2] Offer facts disagree across pages: homepage "included" promises transcripts and AI for every class and pads with the free tracker; toddler page never mentions transcripts; infant page never mentions AI; pricing distinguishes unlimited AI (toddler) from AI (infant). Fix: one canonical facts list rendered identically in the home enrol band, /classes/ rows, class-page facts and pricing; drop free items from "included". Command: /impeccable clarify.
5. [P2] Long measures on the class pages and pricing: .check-list li, .educator p, class FAQ answers (~85-108ch) and pricing FAQ answers (114-128ch) exceed DESIGN.md's 60ch. Fix: max-width ~62ch on those blocks. Command: /impeccable typeset.
