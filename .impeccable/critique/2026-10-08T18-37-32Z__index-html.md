## Anti-Patterns Verdict

LLM: not slop. Four sourced teaching figures (plasticity windows, crying curve, "Words land here", course map), drenched ink, one amber highlighter and commissioned photos make it this brand and no other. Edge-of-lane risks: the editorial-typographic family is the predictable second-order answer for "parenting education that isn't pastel"; one list form (1px ruled rows) carries nearly every section, so the phone page is one long hairline cadence; class pages follow the stock course-sales skeleton (outcomes, syllabus, "If any of this sounds familiar, you're in the right place.", who teaches, FAQ, ✓ checklist beside a price card).

Detector: CLI 9 findings, all single-font (false positive: regex stops at the first & in the Google Fonts URL; computed styles show Atkinson on 183 elements and Besley on 53 on the homepage). Browser overlay injected on 6 pages (headless): dark-glow x4 and gpt-thin-border-wide-shadow on .chat/.nav__menu match DESIGN.md's button-amber, lg and md tokens; low-contrast on the nav and the visual-contrast pass are false positives (oklab background misread; graph-paper grid read as opaque; real ratios 5.85:1 to 14.8:1); text-overflow on the rotated "Arousal" label and tight-leading on the toddler CTA are false positives. Real: line-length on class-page FAQ answers (96-104 characters per line) because styles.css declares max-width twice in .faq-class-item > p and 68ch wins over 62ch. The LLM review missed this (accordions closed).

## Priority Issues

1. [P1] No way to see a lesson before a final-sale purchase. No page on this branch shows a still, clip, transcript excerpt, or player; "final" appears 2-3 times per page; "Can I try the approach before I pay?" answers with articles. Fix: merge PR #84 and make "Watch a lesson free" the infant secondary action (hero, open-now row, enrol card); for the toddler class add a short clip or a written lesson excerpt with its figure; lead the refund answer with the preview and say "final" once, on the price card. Command: /impeccable onboard, then /impeccable clarify.
2. [P1] On phones, "Enroll" lands on a screen with no Enroll button. Class-page sticky bar and hero "Enroll, $49" jump to #enroll; the band opens with H2, lede, a 4-item checklist and "All classes" before the price card. Verified at 375x812: the button's top lands at y=812 (infant) and y=875 (toddler), just off-screen, while the sticky bar steps aside. Fix: below 720px put the price card first, or anchor #enroll on .signup__form with scroll-margin. Command: /impeccable adapt.
3. [P2] The checkout split is explained, not designed. Six Enroll labels ("Enroll", "Enroll, $49", "Enroll now, $49", "Enroll on Thinkific", "Enroll on Thinkific, $49", "Enroll in a class, $49"); ↗ only on the homepage button; toddler hero and header still "Enroll, $49"; "Thinkific" appears 8 times on the toddler page; the 19px platform paragraph (.enrol-fine) is larger than the class meta above it. Fix: one label ("Enroll, $49"), with a quiet per-row line "Opens Thinkific in a new tab ↗" for Class 2; ↗ on every new-tab button; demote the paragraph to per-row notes. Command: /impeccable clarify.
4. [P2] /pricing is a flat wall of equal primaries. Five offers with identical teal primaries (Free, Class 1, Class 2, AI Pro $9/mo, Number Path $34) plus the header CTA; AI Pro and the paid Number Path appear nowhere else, and Number Path sits under "Free tools" in the nav. Fix: classes primary; Free as a link; AI Pro and Number Path under a quiet "Also available"; nav description "Free check-in; full version $34". Command: /impeccable distill.
5. [P2] Sibling pages drift. Toddler syllabus has module blurbs while infant lists all 16 lessons; preschool, family-systems and /pricing keep straight quotes; "Family systems & stress" vs "and"; family-systems "6 modules" vs homepage "in development"; waitlist promises disagree ("early access + free milestone tracker, unsubscribe anytime" vs "the moment one opens, and nothing else"); class-page "Who teaches it" is vaguer than the homepage instructor block; class FAQ answers run 96-104 characters per line (68ch override). Command: /impeccable polish (with /impeccable typeset for the measure).

## Questions to Consider

- What if the first thing a phone visitor could do were watch two minutes of a real lesson?
- If trust is the conversion, why does the site say "All sales are final" more often than it shows what's inside a class? What would you have to believe about the lessons to offer a 14-day refund instead?
- Does the purchase moment need to know about Stripe and Thinkific at all, or could each button carry one quiet line and leave the rest to the FAQ?
