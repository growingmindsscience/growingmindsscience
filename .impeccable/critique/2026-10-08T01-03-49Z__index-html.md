## Anti-Patterns Verdict

LLM: the homepage passes. Captioned SVG figures with named sources, a syllabus-style curriculum, commissioned thumbnails, and specific human copy. Remaining tells: sentence-case kickers above the H1 and AI H2, stock sticky-quote thesis layout, credentials repeated three times. The conversion path fails: /classes/, toddlerhood, birth-to-12-months, /pricing use 135deg gradient product cards, a "Best value" badge, a "Module 01-05" card grid, stock-looking photos (toddler-blocks-4x3.jpg, baby-hands.jpg), text-only heroes, and zero figures.

Detector: CLI 7 findings (single-font x5 = detector bug, it reads only the first Google Fonts family=; numbered-section-markers x1 toddlerhood "Module 01..05", partly valid; design-system-radius x1 serve-and-return 999px on a 44px circle, false positive and dead CSS). Browser overlay on 5 pages: line-length 15 (real: .product-card__promise ~119ch, toddlerhood lists/FAQ 88-102ch), oversized-h1 on serve-and-return (real: clamp max 5.6rem), gpt-thin-border-wide-shadow on .nav__menu (real, minor: 16px radius off scale, shadow off token), dark-glow x4 and .chat shadow (false positives: DESIGN.md tokens), clipped-overflow x3 (false positives: visually-hidden labels and aria-hidden decor), hero-eyebrow-chip on .article-kicker (partly valid), cramped-padding .msg__source (borderline). Homepage overlay results varied between runs.

Fix: size labels to render >=14px (or HTML overlays), mobile variant, keep tails above baseline, add a caption sentence that plasticity tapers rather than switches off. Command: /impeccable typeset (+ clarify for the caption).

[P2] Mobile homepage weight and duplicate chrome: ~15,000px tall at 375; sticky "Browse the classes" bar stays up over #classes and the open menu; chat demo reserves ~600px of blank card before playing (messages hidden until 30% in view, no failsafe).
