# Surface brief: Right Now

Mode: Operate. A stressed parent needs to recognize their situation and find
something usable immediately, often on a phone with one hand available.

Refinement of the existing field-guide world: Newsreader, Inter, Haze ground,
forest ink, quiet ruled choices, and sage mist for a phrase to borrow. Preserve
the minimal header and all five situations. On desktop, put the question beside
the choices; on phones, keep a single reading column. Keep the prescribed 11px
brand descriptor from DESIGN.md's header specification.

Background constraint (Matthew, 2026-11-01): the light page ground is Haze
(midpoint), #F8F9FB. Scope this override to Right Now; preserve dark-mode tokens.

Delight thesis: the parent feels accompanied because the interface makes one
small next step easy, without asking them to absorb a whole explanation first.

Answers show all three existing steps by default, with optional one-step reading
and clear previous/next controls. Emphasize each step's existing opening phrase.
Present the existing spoken phrase on sage mist with a copy control. Keep the
original explanation under “Why this helps” and retain the full-tool link,
Save, Listen, Ask, Print, deep links, and browser Back behavior.

Motion thesis: only the guided step transition deserves authored movement,
a bounded 180ms shift and crossfade that explains changed content. Reduced
motion shows the step immediately. There are no loops or delayed entrances.

Audio constraint (Matthew, 2026-11-01): Listen recordings use the provided text
as their script. Do not change any title, body, sequence, or tryLine in
#router-data. Keep the narration concatenation, punctuation normalization,
ordering, and “Try saying:” prefix identical. Interface-only labels stay outside
the narration. Existing audio hashes: meltdown 3ce62fb1, limit c185f0fd,
sleep 61d656e5, patience 0fa9b2fd, repair acde4214.

Scope excludes the site's branding migration and unrelated working-tree edits.

Release integration preserves the latest From Little Onward metadata, wordmark,
print attribution, safety information, and shared Haze/dark-mode tokens from main.
