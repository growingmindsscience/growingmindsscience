# Claims Library: source check and research, October 2026

A pre-check for Matthew's Tier 4 review of `artifacts/claims/claims.v1.json` (`REVIEW.md`). Nothing in the artifact has changed. Every grade, summary and source edit below is a proposal for Matthew to accept or reject. Per the pipeline, his grade is the grade.

## How this was done

- Each listed source was looked up on PubMed, Crossref or the journal page, and its details were checked.
- Each summary sentence was read against what the source actually reports. Mostly that meant abstracts; full texts were read where they were open.
- Newer or stronger evidence was searched for, and only sources found on a real page were kept.
- All 20 new PubMed IDs proposed below were checked again against PubMed esummary on 2026-10-15, and each matches its author, journal and year.
- **Limit:** the full texts of the AAP *Pediatrics* papers were blocked (403), so those were judged from abstracts.

## Sources to add (all verified)

**sleep-training-attachment**
- Hiscock H et al. Pediatrics 2008;122(3):e621-7. PMID 18762495. Two-year outcomes: no adverse effects; less maternal depression.
- Bilgin A, Wolke D. J Child Psychol Psychiatry 2020;61(11):1184-93. PMID 32155677. Cohort; no attachment harm from "cry it out" in the first 6 months.
- Douglas PS, Hill PS. J Dev Behav Pediatr 2013;34(7):497-507. PMID 24042081. Dissenting review for under 6 months.
- Fix: Price 2012's title is truncated (it ends "...intervention: randomized trial").

**teething-fever**
- Macknin ML et al. Pediatrics 2000;105(4):747-52. PMID 10742315. Prospective; mild temperature rise, not fever over 102°F; warns against attributing illness to teething. Could replace Markman 2009, whose role text could not be verified (no abstract).
- Nemezio MA et al. Int J Clin Pediatr Dent 2017;10(3):293-8. PMID 29104392. Meta-analysis; no overall association, one with rectal readings.
- AAP HealthyChildren.org, "Teething Pain" (updated 2025-07-09). The named-organization position.

**baby-walkers**
- Sims A et al. Pediatrics 2018;142(4):e20174332. PMID 30224365. 230,676 US ER visits 1990 to 2014; supports the AAP call for a ban.
- Pin T et al. Dev Med Child Neurol 2007;49(11):858-67. PMID 17979866. Walker studies are of "generally poor methodological quality."
- Canada Consumer Product Safety Act, Schedule 2 item 3 (the 2004 ban).

**tummy-time**
- Replace the non-citation with Moon RY et al. Pediatrics 2022;150(1):e2022057990. PMID 35726558 (AAP safe-sleep policy).
- WHO. Guidelines on physical activity, sedentary behaviour and sleep for children under 5. 2019. ISBN 978-92-4-155053-6. "At least 30 minutes" prone, spread through the day.
- AAP HealthyChildren.org, "Back to Sleep, Tummy to Play."

**bilingual-confusion**
- Oller DK et al. J Child Lang 1997;24(2):407-25. PMID 9308425. Canonical babbling onset is the same.
- Hoff E et al. J Child Lang 2012;39(1):1-27. PMID 21418730. Per-language lag; total vocabulary comparable.
- Verhoeven E et al. Child Dev 2026 (online Sep 16). PMID 42747409. Synthesis: parental mixing mostly unrelated to vocabulary. It updates Byers-Heinlein's 2013 "jury is still out."

**pacifiers-speech**
- Burr S et al. Folia Phoniatr Logop 2021;73(2):75-88. PMID 32040950. Systematic review; inconclusive; longer sucking duration is the most consistent signal.
- Strutt C et al. Int J Lang Commun Disord 2021;56(3):512-27. PMID 33939239. Most speech outcomes are not associated; frequent daytime use is linked to atypical errors under about 38 months.
- Moon RY et al. 2022, PMID 35726558, for the SIDS sentence.
- Fix: rewrite the role text of Sexton 2009 and Nelson 2012 to what they cover (ear infections, SIDS, dental).

**talking-to-baby**
- Ferjan Ramírez N et al. PNAS 2020;117(7):3484-91. PMID 32015127. RCT from 6 months: coaching raised turns and language growth.
- Donnelly S, Kidd E. Child Dev 2021;92(2):609-25. PMID 33547640. Independent (non-LENA-Foundation) longitudinal support for turns and vocabulary.
- Anderson NJ et al. Child Dev 2021;92(2):484-501. PMID 33521953. Meta-analysis: quality r = .33, quantity r = .20.

**screen-time-language**
- Madigan S et al. JAMA Pediatr 2020;174(7):665-75. PMID 32202633. The language meta-analysis: screen time r = -0.14, background TV r = -0.19, co-viewing r = +0.16.
- Kirkorian HL et al. Child Dev 2009;80(5):1350-9. PMID 19765004. Background TV reduces parent-child interaction.
- Brushe ME et al. JAMA Pediatr 2024;178(4):369-75. PMID 38436942. LENA recordings: more screen time, fewer words and turns.
- Optional: Al-Jbouri E et al. JAMA Pediatr 2026, PMID 42804186 (umbrella review).

**shared-reading**
- Dowdall N et al. Child Dev 2020;91(2):e383-99. PMID 30737957. 19 RCTs: expressive d = 0.41, receptive d = 0.26.
- Noble C et al. Educ Res Rev 2019;28:100290. DOI 10.1016/j.edurev.2019.100290. Smaller effects, negligible against active controls (exact g values seen only in an abstract excerpt).
- Klass P et al. Pediatrics 2024;154(6):e2024069091. PMID 39342415. AAP literacy promotion report.

**baby-led-weaning**
- Fangupo LJ et al. Pediatrics 2016;138(4):e20160772. PMID 27647715. BLISS choking outcome.
- Daniels L et al. BMJ Open 2018;8(6):e019036. PMID 29950456. Iron no different with high-iron advice.
- Márquez-Díaz RR et al. Nutr Rev 2026. PMID 41528760. Systematic review of 4 RCTs.
