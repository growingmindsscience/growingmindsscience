What is fixed across all three

  • Tokens are the guidelines’ own hex values. Newsreader over Inter, the preferred pair. Calm 6 to 10px corners, quiet rules, small figure numbers.
  • Homepage story in the guidelines’ order: hero, Lens, age, three dimensions, hard moments, classes, tools and evidence, trust and invitation.
  • “See what’s growing.” leads the hero. “The first five years, understood.” leads the classes. “Mental models, not scripts.” leads trust. “Less pressure. Better explanations.” leads the close.
  • Photographs are the four generated pictures already in the repository plus the founder portrait. No stock, no silhouettes. Diagrams are a relationship map (A) and a developmental timeline (C), both labelled conceptual.
  • The Lens works: four steps, keyboard tablist, no-script fallback (all panels shown), reduced motion honoured. Its science is still marked as awaiting source review.
  • Not built yet: the Explore hub, inner pages, the real header behaviour (theme toggle, tools menu). These pages are compositions, not the shipped homepage.
