How old is your child?

A lot of what helps depends on age. Pick a period, and you will find the moments and topics that matter in it.

Birth to 1 Foundations Attachment, the senses, and the rhythm of back-and-forth. Serve and return, not flashcards, builds the architecture. 1 to 2 First words, first steps Understanding races ahead of speech. Looking at something together is how words get attached to things. 2 to 3 Autonomy and big feelings “No” is a milestone, not defiance. Big feelings outrun the brakes while the brakes are still being built. 3 to 4 The imagination years Pretend play is serious cognitive work: two realities at once, other perspectives, social roles rehearsed. 4 to 5 Self-control, slowly Working memory, flexible thinking and impulse control are under construction, on a wide and normal range.
